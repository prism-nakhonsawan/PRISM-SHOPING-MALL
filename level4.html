People have always wondered whether bird superstitions are true. Some people believe them because crows often appear after a battle, and this makes the birds seem like signs of death. However, others argue that these are just old wives' tales; for example, swallows eat insects simply because they are hungry, not because they bring luck. In my opinion, some superstitions may hide a real reason, so they are worth studying rather than laughing at. Therefore, we should think carefully before we dismiss an old belief.

How your paragraph is scored

PRISM 5 dimensions · each scored 1-4 / เกณฑ์ PRISM 5 มิติ ให้ระดับ 1-4
TRTask Response

4 High Both views + clear opinion, all on topic. / สองมุม + ความเห็นชัด ตรงประเด็น
